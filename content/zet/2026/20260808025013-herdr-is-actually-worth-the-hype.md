# herdr is actually worth the hype

The Green Mile meme "I'm tired boss" is how I feel about most multiplexer apps these days.

I am a zellij mam after years of futzying around in tmux configs i settled on zellij brcause its 80% works out of the box. I run most agents in there own panes split into tab groups. Works well even now.

After dismissing it for a while I thought if its getting this hyped but people who aren't usually hype lords - if you didnt go openclaw retarded you pass my benchmark - I should try it.

And, after a week. I am happy with it. I do like that you can see all agents and their status easily. I love the extensions like code review. I love that I can ssh into my machine from my phone to see stuff, or spawn a task at a whim.

Will it remove zellij. No I dont think so but its another great tool in the box to leverage.
