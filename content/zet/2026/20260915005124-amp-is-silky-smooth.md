# amp is silky smooth

[amp](https://ampcode.com) is the most silky smooth harness I've discovered so far.

What a lovingly crafted, well architectured and feature packed tool.

I've set up my OpenCode Go and Codex subs. Now inference is "free" and the only thing thats costing me is Orb usage. 

To reduce costs I just asked "Puck" its meta harness thingy how I could do it and it gave me all the info, and then did it for me. 
The app itself functions so well, seamlessly jumping from the orbs to puck with deeplinking that actually works. 

Going to give it a couple of weeks on smaller projects to get a real feel for it but so far its superior to Codex or Claude Code in terms of features, ease of use and thoughtfulness.

Now I just need to see how it handles writing code - though I expect it will do very well having used Amp in the past!
